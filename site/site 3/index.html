Four ingredients. Nothing hidden.

All the protein comes from roasted chana, soy nuts and peanuts, the classic protein-rich namkeen ingredients, not from added powder. Here's the full recipe by weight and the maths behind the number on the pack.

  • 50%Roasted chana22.5 g protein per 100 g
  • 25%Dry-roasted soy nutsAbout 38.5 g protein per 100 g
  • 20%Roasted peanutsAbout 26 g protein per 100 g
  • 5%Masala, salt and a little oilFlavour only, counted as zero protein

(0.50 × 22.5) + (0.25 × 38.5) + (0.20 × 26) ≈ 26 g protein per 100 g

Questions

Comparing snacks? Read the best high-protein snacks in India or how much protein is in roasted chana.

Can I buy DumDana right now?

Not yet. DumDana is a market test run by a college team. We're checking whether people want a high-protein roasted namkeen before making it. Join the waitlist and you'll hear first if it goes ahead.

Why ₹20 and ₹30, and not ₹10?

We worked out a ₹10 pack first. After the shop's margin, the distributor's margin and 5% GST, a ₹10 pack leaves the maker about ₹6.50, so the pack would have to shrink to about 25 g. We'd rather keep the recipe and the protein and charge a fair price. The ₹30 pack (70 g, about 18 g of protein) is the best value.

Where do the protein numbers come from?

We calculated them from published nutrition data: Indian food composition tables for roasted chana, and USDA data for soy nuts and peanuts. The mix works out to about 26 g of protein per 100 g. On the packs we round down (7.8 g to 7.5 g, 18.2 g to 18 g) to leave a safety margin. No value goes on a real pack until an accredited lab has tested it.

How is DumDana different from protein chips?

Protein chips give about 25 g of protein per 100 g and cost about ₹50 for 10 g of protein. DumDana gives about 26 g per 100 g from whole roasted chana, soy and peanuts, and its ₹30 pack works out to about ₹16.70 for the same 10 g.

Is it fried?

No. The chana, soy nuts and peanuts are dry-roasted. A little oil is used only to make the masala stick.

What are the allergens?

DumDana contains peanuts and soy. Please skip it if you're allergic to either.

Will it help me build muscle or lose weight?

We don't make health claims. DumDana is a namkeen with more protein than most snacks. How it fits into your diet is up to you and, if you need advice, a qualified nutritionist.

When will DumDana launch?

There's no date yet. If the market test shows enough people want it, the next step is a small sample batch and a lab test. Waitlist members hear first, and we'll tell you honestly if we decide not to go ahead.
