How much protein is in roasted chana?

Published 25 October 2026 by the DumDana team. 5 minute read.

Roasted chana has about 22.5 g of protein per 100 g. A 30 g handful gives you roughly 6.7 g of protein, for about ₹3 at wholesale prices. That makes it one of the cheapest ready-to-eat protein sources in India.

Protein in roasted chana, by portion

The figures below use 22.5 g of protein per 100 g, from Indian food composition data for roasted Bengal gram (the small, dark desi chana sold roasted in most markets).

PortionWeightProtein
A small handful30 g6.7 g
A katori (small bowl)50 g11.3 g
100 g100 g22.5 g

Handful sizes vary, so weigh it once if you're tracking protein closely.

Roasted chana vs peanuts vs soy nuts

All three are roasted, cheap and sold loose at most kirana shops. Here's how they compare on protein:

SnackProtein per 100 gGood to know
Dry-roasted soy nuts≈38.5 gThe most protein of the three. USDA lists roasted soy at about 35–43 g; we use the middle value.
Roasted peanuts≈26 gTasty and filling, but much higher in fat.
Roasted chana22.5 gLow in fat, very cheap, and the crunchiest base.

Mixing them works well. Our DumDana recipe (50% chana, 25% soy nuts, 20% peanuts, 5% masala) comes out at about 26 g of protein per 100 g: more than chana alone, with less fat than peanuts alone.

What 10 g of protein costs

Roasted chana sells wholesale for roughly ₹80–120 per kg (IndiaMART listings, October 2026). At those prices, 10 g of protein from roasted chana costs about ₹3.60–5.30 before any packaging or shop margin. Compare that with packaged protein snacks:

  • Protein chips: about ₹50 for 10 g of protein (SuperYou, ₹50 for a 40 g pack).
  • DumDana ₹20 pack (target): about ₹13 for 10 g of protein, packed and sold at a shop.
  • Loose roasted chana: about ₹4–5 for 10 g of protein, if you buy it loose and portion it yourself.

Loose chana is the cheapest option of all. A pack makes sense when you want it portioned, flavoured and sold next to the chai.

Can you call roasted chana "high protein"?

Under the FSSAI (Advertising and Claims) Regulations, 2018, a solid food needs at least 20% of the protein RDA per 100 g to be called "high in protein". Compliance guides translate that to roughly 12 g of protein per 100 g. Roasted chana, at 22.5 g, clears it easily. Packaged brands still need an accredited lab report before printing the claim.

Quick answers

How much protein is in 100 g of roasted chana?

About 22.5 g, according to Indian food composition data for roasted Bengal gram.

How much protein is in a handful of roasted chana?

A 30 g handful has about 6.7 g of protein. A 50 g bowl has about 11 g.

Which has more protein: roasted chana, peanuts or soy nuts?

Soy nuts have the most (about 38.5 g per 100 g), then peanuts (about 26 g), then roasted chana (22.5 g). Chana has much less fat than peanuts.
