Best high-protein snacks in India, compared by price

Published 28 October 2026 by the DumDana team

The best-value high-protein snacks in India are roasted soy nuts, roasted chana and peanuts. They give 22–38 g of protein per 100 g for about ₹3–5 per 10 g of protein when bought loose. Protein chips give similar protein but cost about ₹50 for 10 g.

Searches for "high protein" in India have roughly quadrupled since 2021 on Google Trends, and "best high protein snacks" is one of the fastest-rising related searches. Here's how the common vegetarian options compare on protein and price, with a source for every number.

Best protein snacks for work and college

  • For your desk drawer: a sealed pack of roasted chana or soy nuts needs no fridge and no prep.
  • For the 5 pm chai break: a masala chana or soy mix replaces fried namkeen without losing the crunch.
  • On a tight budget: buy roasted chana loose and portion it into small boxes; it's the cheapest protein on this list.
  • If you want zero effort: packaged snacks cost more per gram of protein, so compare the "cost of 10 g protein" column before you buy.

How to read a protein label quickly

  1. Find protein per 100 g on the back of the pack. Above 12 g is usually treated as "high protein" for solid foods in India (source).
  2. Check the pack weight, then work out protein per pack.
  3. Divide the price by the protein to get the cost per gram. This is the fairest way to compare a ₹10 pack with a ₹50 one.

Quick answers

What is the cheapest high-protein snack in India?

Loose roasted chana, soy nuts and peanuts bought by weight are the cheapest, at about ₹3–5 for 10 g of protein at wholesale prices.

Which vegetarian snack has the most protein?

Of common Indian snacks, dry-roasted soy nuts have the most, about 38.5 g of protein per 100 g.

Are protein chips worth it?

Protein chips give about 25 g of protein per 100 g, but cost about ₹50 for 10 g of protein. Roasted chana, soy nuts and peanuts give similar protein for much less.
